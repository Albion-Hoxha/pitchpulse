## How to record

**App settings** (link: https://pitchpulse.livelyfield-1dcb67cb.polandcentral.azurecontainerapps.io)
- Open the app 1 minute early so it is awake. Browser full screen (F11), zoom 100%, 1920×1080.
- **Match 7**, **Speed 60×** (one match minute ≈ one real second), **Language: Albanian**, **🔊 Voice** on → Kick off. The goal comes at ~0:33 after kick-off.
- Record each scene as its own clip; we cut them together afterwards. Pause the match when needed.
- For the "same goal, two fans" scene: play match 7 twice, once with **Supporting: Riverside Athletic**, once with **Northbridge FC**, and record the goal line both times.
- For the speed indicator: **Focus metric: Pressing**; Moreau's 114 km/h shot and O'Connell's 120 km/h shot appear in match 7.

**Tools (free)**
- Screen recording: **Xbox Game Bar** (Win + G → record) or **OBS Studio**.
- Editing and captions: **Clipchamp** (built into Windows) – add auto-captions in English.
- Voice-over: record in Clipchamp or on a phone in a quiet room.

**Rules to respect**
- Under 2:00 in total.
- No copyrighted music (no music at all, or only royalty-free tracks from Clipchamp/YouTube Audio Library).
- No real Premier League or club logos, players or footage.
- Upload to **YouTube** as **Public** or **Unlisted** (not Private), title: *PitchPulse – AI Broadcast Director | Inside the Game Hackathon*.
